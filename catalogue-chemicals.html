Powder Polycarboxylic Acid SuperplasticizerSeries KYD
Water-Reducing KYD-A
Product Features

It demonstrates excellent water reduction ratio and superior dispersion performance, making it ideal for applications requiring high flowability.

Applied Range

Applications include concrete, grouting material, self-leveling compound, waterproof mortar, repair mortar, and high-ductility concrete.

Product Parameter
Appearance: White to pale yellow powder · Bulk density: 400–700 kg/m³ · Moisture content: ≤5% · pH (20% liquid): 4–6 · Concrete water reduction rate: ≥25%
Packing Specifications
25 kg/bag · 40 bags/ton bag · 750 kg/ton bag (bulk)
Usage and Dosage
  • In ready-mix applications, the materials are added according to the mix ratio and thoroughly mixed.
  • When liquefying, prepare clean water, activate the stirring device, and slowly add the substance to the water at a uniform rate, stirring until complete dissolution.
  • Recommended dosage range: 0.20–0.45% of the cementitious material content. Optimal proportion determined through testing based on raw materials and engineering requirements.
  • When raw materials are changed, conduct new suitability tests and determine the dosage.
  • Do not use in combination with naphthalene-based, melamine-based, or aminosulfonate superplasticizers.
