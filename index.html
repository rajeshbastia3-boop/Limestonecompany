About Limestone Company

India-Based Aluminium, Polymer & Chemical Importer & Supplier

We are an India-based aluminium extrusion trading and sourcing company engaged in supplying high-quality aluminium profiles, tubes, and industrial sections for construction, solar, and engineering applications.

We work closely with reputed aluminium extrusion manufacturers across India to source products as per customer requirements, drawings, and technical specifications. Our focus is on delivering reliable quality, competitive pricing, and timely supply for both domestic and export markets.

We are also one of India's leading importers and suppliers of PVC Resin — catering to pipe manufacturers, cable compounders, profile extruders and flexible film producers across the country. Our PVC resin portfolio covers suspension and emulsion grades sourced from certified global manufacturers.

In the Polyolefin segment, we supply Polypropylene (PP) and Polyethylene (PE) grades for packaging, injection moulding, pipe extrusion and film applications — backed by consistent quality and competitive pricing.

Our Industrial Chemicals division covers a comprehensive range including PCE Admixtures, Silica Fume, Calcium Nitrate, Sodium Gluconate and specialty additives — serving construction chemical formulators, RMC plants and industrial processors.

Our strength lies in efficient sourcing, a strong global and domestic supplier network, and customer-focused service that ensures the right product reaches the right place on time.
