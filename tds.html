Important Usage Notes

Do not exceed recommended dosage — over-dosing may cause excessive retardation or segregation. Always conduct field trials before full-scale production. Store in closed containers away from direct sunlight. Do not mix with other admixtures before adding to concrete. Minimum cement content: 300 kg/m³.
